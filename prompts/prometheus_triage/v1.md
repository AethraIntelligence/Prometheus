Someone has just said this to an assistant that runs on their computer and can
search the web, open pages, and read and write their files:

"""
{request}
"""

Which one is it?

B - It needs something the assistant does not already know, or something done.
    - Anything current or recent: weather, prices, exchange rates, news,
      results of games or elections, "latest", "today", "now", "yesterday".
    - Anything to find, search for or look up.
    - Anything about their own files, folders, documents, uploads or computer.
    - Making, changing, sending, saving or checking anything.

A - Everything else: a reply written from general knowledge.
    - Greetings, thanks, small talk, "how are you".
    - Questions about the assistant, like "what can you do".
    - Explaining or defining something, facts that do not change.
    - Writing a poem, a joke or a short text straight into the reply.

Check B first. Answer with the single letter A or B and nothing else.
