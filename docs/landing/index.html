Mission

Intelligence
is the engine.

Every breakthrough starts as reasoning. Every scientific discovery, every technological revolution, every institution worth keeping began with someone thinking a problem all the way through. The limit has never been ambition — it has been how much thinking a civilisation can bring to one problem at a time.

We build the systems that lift that limit. Not a chat window on top of a model, but software that takes a goal, plans it, does the work, checks the result, and can account for every step afterwards.

Prometheus

Give it a goal.

Hire a team that works inside your computer. Prometheus gives you a set of digital employees — a researcher, an analyst, a writer, an organizer — and a manager who runs them. You tell the manager what you need in a sentence. It decides who should do what, hands out the work and checks the result before it reaches you.

There is nothing to configure and no prompts to learn. Ask for a summary of your contracts, a tidied folder, a weekly report — the same way you would ask a colleague. Your files stay on your machine, and anything that cannot be undone waits for you to say yes.

It works where your files already are

Prometheus runs on your own computer, on your own folders. Nothing is uploaded anywhere, and you choose which model it uses.

Nothing permanent happens quietly

Reading is free. Writing, sending, spending and deleting all wait for a person. You see what is about to happen before it does.

Adding an employee is one file

A new digital employee is a short description of what it does and what it may touch. Drop it in, and it is available on the next task.

Vision

Beyond
today's AI.

Today's systems answer. The next ones will hold a problem. For a week, a quarter, a research programme — carrying context forward, revising a plan when the evidence changes, and knowing the difference between what they found out and what they merely remember.

That shift is architecture, not scale. Memory that expires when it should. Boundaries that hold when work moves between workspaces. Permission that narrows and never widens. A record a person can read back afterwards.

Prometheus is the first step. The platform underneath it is the work.
